Verse finds, syncs and saves lyrics for songs from Spotify, SoundCloud or your own audio files, and can transcribe a song's vocals into word-timed lyrics on your PC.

Download for Windows

Free · Windows 10/11 (64-bit) · Updates itself

Made for singing along

Karaoke view

Full-screen lyrics with each word filling in as it's sung, a countdown through instrumentals, and Sing along mode that plays the instrumental only.

AI lyrics reconstruction

Isolates the vocals and transcribes them into word-timed, editable lyrics. The free engine runs entirely on your PC; nothing is uploaded.

Sync with AI

Already have the lyrics? Verse times your own text to the song from the audio, keeping your lines and sections.

Paste a link or open a file

Spotify and SoundCloud links, a song name, or MP3/FLAC/M4A files. Lyrics come from LRCLIB, with song details from Genius.

Embed lyrics into audio

Writes synced lyrics into a copy of your MP3, FLAC, OGG or M4A. The original file is never touched. Export LRC, SRT, JSON or TXT too.

Your whole library

Import a music folder, analyse songs missing lyrics in the background, and back up or restore your library. GPU-accelerated on any DirectX 12 card.
