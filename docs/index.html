AI lyrics reconstruction

No lyrics online?
Verse listens.

It separates the vocals from the music and writes down what was actually sung, with a timestamp on every word. The free engine runs on your own PC. Nothing is uploaded and no account is needed.

Only the audio can change a word.
Published lyrics and AI review can only suggest. A word is replaced only when a second listen hears it too.
01
Isolate the vocals
A vocal-separation model strips the music away, on your graphics card when it can.
02
Transcribe the lead
Whisper writes down what was sung, verbatim, with word-level timing.
03
Catch backing vocals
Harmonies and ad-libs are found separately and kept in parentheses.
04
Lock every word in place
Each word is tightened to the vocal energy, in strict time order.
05
Listen again
Unsure words get an independent second pass before anything changes.
06
Score the confidence
Low-confidence words are flagged for you instead of guessed.
07
Shape the lines
Words are split into lines at natural pauses and checked as valid LRC.
